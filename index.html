Traditional vs. Generative AI: What You Need to Know

Dec 14, 2025 · 3 min read

Most people think AI started with ChatGPT. But we've been using AI since the 90s — the difference is that older AI was narrowly focused, while today's AI can understand, create, and converse.

Traditional AI relies on pre-defined patterns and data. It's exceptional at analysis and execution, but has zero creativity. Think Google Search, YouTube recommendations, or spam filters — all pattern matchers.

Generative AI doesn't just recognize patterns — it creates. Given what it learned from billions of text examples, it generates entirely new content: essays, code, images, strategies. Tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and GitHub Copilot all fall here.

The real question isn't which is better — it's knowing which one you're using and for what. Traditional AI excels at classification and prediction. Generative AI excels at creation and explanation.

How LLMs and Neural Networks Actually Work

Dec 17, 2025 · 4 min read

Here's what no one tells you: AI doesn't "think." It plays a game. Specifically, it plays a next-word prediction game at massive scale.

When you write "I'm going to ____", an LLM assigns probabilities to every possible next word based on billions of training examples — and picks the highest-probability fit for the context. That's the entire trick. It's sequential prediction, done extraordinarily well.

Under the hood, a Neural Network does the heavy lifting. Like the human brain has neurons, AI has nodes — organized in three layers: Input (raw data goes in), Hidden layers (patterns are extracted), and Output (the result comes out). The more hidden layers, the deeper the learning — which is where "Deep Learning" gets its name.

Real-world examples: Image Recognition (Google Lens, Face ID), Self-Driving Cars (analyzing camera + radar feeds in real time), and AI match analysis in sports (tracking passes, predicting outcomes).

🤖 The magic isn't mysterious — it's statistics at a mind-bending scale.

The $100M Secret Behind Training Models Like GPT-4

Jan 4, 2026 · 4 min read

Training GPT-4 on your personal laptop would take thousands of years. That's not an exaggeration — it's why companies spend $100M+ to do it in 3 months.

The staggering numbers: 20,000 NVIDIA A100 GPUs (each priced like a new car), running for 3 straight months, consuming enough electricity to power a football stadium for 9 years.

The training process has 3 phases: Pre-training (the model reads the entire internet and learns to predict the next word), Fine-tuning (it learns to be helpful and polite through Q&A scenarios), and RLHF (humans rank its answers and teach it which responses feel most natural — that's what makes ChatGPT feel like a conversation).

But even after all this, the model still hallucinates facts, carries bias from training data, has a knowledge cutoff, and has no real moral compass — it's pure math.

🚀 Extraordinary power. Still needs a human in the loop.

Gaming & Mental Health Analysis

Overview

A complete data analytics project investigating the correlation between gaming habits and mental health indicators across 1,000 player records — from raw data to interactive dashboards.

🎯 The Problem

Understanding the actual statistical correlation between gaming habits and mental health indicators (like focus and stress) across 1,000 player records — without any clean, structured data to start from.

💡 Approach

Applied statistical outlier detection (Mean ± 3 Standard Deviations) to clean the dataset, then engineered features like Physical Pain Index and Addiction Risk scores. Built a Star Schema with 6 dimension tables and a 3-page interactive dashboard covering Gaming, Health, and Addiction dimensions.

Analytical Process

Raw Data → Cleaning → EDA → Statistical Analysis → Data Modeling → Dashboard → Insights

Technologies

SQL Python Excel Power BI Tableau Statistical Analysis Data Modeling

📈 Business Impact

Identified safe gaming thresholds backed by statistical evidence. The dashboard directly correlates gaming hours with sleep disruption frequency and academic productivity loss — enabling wellness mentors to give data-driven lifestyle recommendations instead of guesswork.

Customer Churn Data Warehouse & ETL Pipeline

🎯 The Problem

Customer behavior was scattered across three disconnected sources — support tickets, offers, and product usage — making it slow and complex to answer the one question that mattered most: which customers were at risk of leaving, and why.

💡 Architecture & Approach

Built an end-to-end pipeline (NiFi → PySpark → Hive) on a Medallion Architecture, unifying all customer activity into a single Star Schema fact table instead of separate tables per source — enabling fast, unified queries on customer behavior. Engineered Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and average ticket resolution time directly into the warehouse as churn-prediction features, and partitioned a 120,000+ row fact table by activity type and year for faster analytical queries.

Pipeline Architecture

4 Source Tables (Tickets, Offers, Usage, Customers) ↓ Apache NiFi — Ingestion ↓ HDFS — Raw Storage ↓ PySpark — ETL (Medallion: Bronze → Silver → Gold) ↓ Hive — Star Schema (120K+ rows, partitioned) ↓ Analytics & Churn Prediction

Technologies

PySpark Hive HDFS Apache NiFi SQL Data Warehousing

📈 Business Impact

The unified activity model lets retention teams identify high-value, at-risk customers in a single query — instead of piecing together tickets, offers, and usage separately — and prioritize retention offers where they matter most, before a customer actually churns.

Real-Time Order & Payment Streaming Pipeline

🎯 The Problem

Batch reporting meant fraud, inventory, and analytics teams were always working with stale data — a payment mismatch or fraudulent transaction could go unnoticed for hours before a nightly batch job ever surfaced it.

💡 Architecture & Approach

Built a real-time ingestion pipeline (Kafka → Spark Structured Streaming → HDFS → Hive) that simulates live order and payment events across two Kafka topics, then performs a stream-to-stream join with watermarking to match each order to its payment within a 5-second window — flagging under/overpayments the moment they happen instead of hours later. Automated startup, shutdown, and checkpoint-based failure recovery via shell scripts.

Streaming Architecture

Orders Producer Payments Producer
↓ Apache Kafka (2 Topics) ↓ Spark Structured Streaming ↓ Stream-to-Stream Join + Watermarking ↓ Fraud Detection (Under/Overpayment) ↓
HDFS Hive

Technologies

Apache Kafka Spark Structured Streaming HDFS Hive

📈 Business Impact

Reduces payment-mismatch detection latency from a 12-hour batch cycle to seconds, and the pipeline recovers automatically from producer or Spark failures without losing or duplicating a single event — critical for a fraud-detection system that can't afford blind spots.
